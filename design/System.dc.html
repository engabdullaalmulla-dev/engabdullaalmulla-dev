DRAWN LIKE A SPORTS MANGA
Everyone in the Gulf grew up on كابتن ماجد — the 80s football manga, dubbed here as Captain Majid. This borrows the language of that era: halftone screentone, speed lines, inked panels, impact lettering. Not its characters or artwork, which belong to their creators.
