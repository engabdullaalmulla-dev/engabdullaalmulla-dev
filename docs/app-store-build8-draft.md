## English description

Paste the following plain text into the description field. It is below Apple's 4,000-character limit. The promotional-text field below has a separate 170-character limit.
```text
A little café. Familiar faces. A story you help write.

Open your own café on a UAE shopping street in 1994. Choose today's menu, welcome your regulars and decide what kind of place you want to build.

YOUR FIRST IN-GAME YEAR IS FREE
Play from 1 January to 31 December 1994 at your own pace. This is time inside the game: you decide when each day advances. One optional purchase unlocks the full game, continuing the same café into later years and family generations. No subscription or recurring charges.

MAKE EACH DAY YOURS
Choose recipes for a menu board with limited space. Match the season and your guests' tastes, pick a room arrangement, choose suppliers and improve the café with permanent tools and seating. Create house recipes by combining ingredients, flavours and temperatures.

GET TO KNOW THE PEOPLE
Remember a favourite cup. Welcome a familiar face. Good service builds lasting relationships, and some earlier story choices open different situations later. Keep the moments that matter in the family book.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
Serve guests yourself or let the team handle the day. Advance ordinary days immediately when you want to reach the next opportunity. Optional daily challenges add small goals and permanent keepsakes. No real-time waiting timers, energy meter or attendance streak to protect.

CONTINUE THE FAMILY STORY
With the full-game unlock, explore later neighbourhood chapters and pass the keys to the next generation. Recipes, improvements and memories stay with the café. Save different cafés and return to the one you want to continue.

MADE FOR A QUIET MOMENT
Hand-painted artwork, music, sound effects and optional haptics. English and Arabic, including right-to-left layouts. Adjust text size, appearance and reduced motion in Settings.

Gameplay works offline. No game account, ads or analytics. Your café saves on your device, and you can export a backup file. An internet connection is needed for purchases and purchase restoration through Apple. Restoring a purchase restores full-game access; importing your save restores your café's progress.
```

## App Review notes — English

Paste after confirming these paths against the signed build selected for review. No game-account credentials are needed. Contact information is entered separately in App Store Connect.

```text
Café Life is an offline café simulation with English and Arabic interfaces. No game-account sign-in is required.

The free opening is the café's first IN-GAME year, 1 January–31 December 1994. It is not a 12-month real-world trial. Players advance time themselves, with no real-time wait. One non-consumable purchase, com.almulla.cafelife.fullgame, unlocks continuation into later years and family generations. There are no subscriptions, ads or consumable purchases.

To review the purchase without completing the free year:
1. Enter a café and owner name, then tap “Open my café”.
2. Open Settings with the gear button.
3. Tap “The full Café Life”.
4. Tap “Unlock everything” followed by the localized App Store price. Apple presents the purchase confirmation.
5. “Restore purchases” is on the same screen.

To inspect the free-year boundary, use Settings > “Let the team take over.” > “Run 30 game days” repeatedly. The game stops at the end of the free year and settles its last day before requiring the full-game unlock to continue. The café remains saved. Export, saved cafés and starting another free café remain available.

To change language, use Settings > Language. Arabic uses right-to-left layouts. Music, sound effects and haptics have separate settings.

Gameplay and progress storage are on-device. Apple services handle the purchase and restoration. A save file contains café progress, not purchase ownership: importing a later-year save does not bypass the verified full-game entitlement. Restore Purchases restores access; importing a save restores progress.
```
