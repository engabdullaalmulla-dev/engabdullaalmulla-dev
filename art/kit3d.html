Why a café is the easy case

Almost everything you serve is rotationally symmetric, and that is the one class of object procedural modelling nails without a sculptor.
Lathe, not sculptA cup, glass, saucer, bottle and jug are each a profile curve spun 360°. I write the profile as numbers; the mesh follows.
Light does the shadingThe work a painter would do by hand — highlight, falloff, contact shadow — is done by the renderer, identically, for every asset.
Every angle is freeChange the camera and you have a new pose. In 2D that is a redraw; here it is one number.
Materials are sharedOne porcelain, one glass, one pastry. Twelve objects cannot drift out of style because they share the same five materials.
BakeableRender once at build time to a sprite atlas and the phone draws flat 2D at runtime — the look of 3D at the cost of 2D.
ParametricA taller glass is one number. A whole new drink is a profile and a colour, not a commission.

What changed versus the flat kit: the shading is no longer my problem. In the vector kit every highlight was a shape I placed by hand, and consistency depended on me obeying my own rules.
